## The Future is an Ephemeral Surface
In the near future, apps may no longer exist as fixed icons on our devices—instead, AI will dynamically generate ephemeral interfaces tailored to each task, blending voice, gesture, and contextual awareness to summon personalized layouts that vanish once their purpose is fulfilled, redefining not just how we interact with software but how software itself is conceived, built, and compensated.

## Paying for ideas
In the coming era of ephemeral software, your phone will no longer host apps or even a fixed operating system — it will simply be a responsive surface where an AI runtime instantly generates whatever tool or environment you need, then dissolves it when finished. Your data and preferences, signed and encrypted in the cloud, provide continuity across devices, while smart contracts on a blockchain ensure that the ideas behind every generated artifact are attributed and fairly compensated. Royalties decay over time, freeing older innovations into the commons and sustaining a living, self-renewing ecosystem where users pay for capability, creators earn for contribution, and software itself becomes transient — a shimmering, intelligent surface between thought and execution.

## The Rise of Sovereign AI: Why Your Digital Twin Will Be Your Most Valuable Asset
This article explores the emerging paradigm of **sovereign AI agents**—personalised digital twins trained on an individual’s private cognitive artefacts such as diaries, lab notes, emails, and decision-making traces. Unlike foundation models trained on public internet data, these agents are deeply aligned with a person’s unique reasoning style, enabling them to act as trusted cognitive avatars. Sovereign agents are private, performative, and unreplicable, offering new opportunities for expert licensing, decentralised knowledge markets, and personal empowerment. The article calls for legal and technical infrastructures to protect cognitive sovereignty and frames the shift as a move from mass AI to individual intelligence at scale.
