# The Future Is an Ephemeral Surface

*Working notes on where the future seems headed, and where it ought to go.*  
*Field reports from a possible future.*

## September 2026

- [What If the Singularity Has a Terminal Velocity?](on_self_improvement.md) \
  Recursive self-improvement may not accelerate forever: delayed feedback, experimental bottlenecks, and reality’s own clock could turn an intelligence explosion into a race against friction.

- [The Most Important AI Technology Might Be an Institution](the_institution.md) \
  AI’s future will depend not only on what machines can do, but on the institutions humans build to preserve agency, constrain power, and govern systems capable of governing others.

- [The Strange Cognitive Tax of Working With AI](codependency.md) \
  AI-assisted work can increase capability and concentration while weakening memory, self-sufficiency, and the connection between producing something and understanding it.


## June 2026


- [After The Ranking Machine](after_the_ranking_machine.md)  
  A proposal for replacing the single-minded pursuit of intelligence and optimization with a richer hierarchy of human goods: life, agency, fallibility, plurality, and inheritance.

- [The Ranking Machine](the_ranking_machine.md)  
  A history of the scales societies use to rank people and possibilities—and a warning that AI could become the newest authority at the top of one.

- [The Teacher's New Superpower Isn't AI Grading—It's AI Insight](ai_for_teachers.md)  
  The most valuable classroom AI may not grade students or replace teachers, but reveal patterns in understanding so teachers can respond with greater precision and care.


## May 2026

- [The Future of Work-Life Balance](resting.md)  
  As work becomes continuous and adaptive, rest becomes a core capability: the rhythm that allows attention, creativity, identity, and intelligence to recover.


## April 2026

- [The Interface That Isn't There](the_end_of_apps.md)  
  When AI generates interfaces around each task, apps give way to ephemeral surfaces—and interface design moves from fixed screens toward semantics, permissions, trust, and memory.

- [The Internet of Mobility](the_internet_of_mobility.md)  
  A proposal for transport’s packet moment: personal mobility pods moving through shared carriers and infrastructure, coordinated by protocols rather than locked to one vehicle or route.

## March 2026

- [The Fifth Cognitive Revolution](cognitive_revolution.md)  
  From language and writing to printing and the internet, each cognitive revolution changed how knowledge moves. AI adds a new step: knowledge can now adapt itself to the mind receiving it.

- [The Social Contract for Artificial Intelligence](ai_social_contract.md)  
  AI is becoming infrastructure, yet its direction is still set largely by market incentives. This essay proposes governance built around public goals, measurable outcomes, human agency, and citizen participation.

- [Learning to Surf the Future](surfing.md)  
  In a world where change is no longer an interruption but the environment itself, adulthood’s central skill shifts from maintaining stability to adapting, recovering, and finding balance in motion.

## January 2026

- [The Whisper Layer: AIs as Invisible Companions](audio.md)  
  Directional audio and ambient sensing could turn voice interfaces into private side-channels, allowing an AI companion to follow you across rooms without becoming public noise.

- [The Soft-Shell Exosuit: A Robot That Wears You Back](robots.md)  
  A shape-shifting exosuit could amplify strength and mobility when worn, then become a standalone helper—part tool, part trainer, and part companion.

- [The Swarm Tool: How Modular Robots Will Rebuild the Factory in Real Time](factory_robots.md)  
  Modular robotic parts could replace fixed factory stations with a reconfigurable swarm that assembles the right tool, vehicle, or micro-drone team for each job.

## December 2025

- [When Everything Looks Real, Trust Becomes a User Interface Problem](trust.md)  
  When synthetic media becomes indistinguishable from capture, trust moves from appearance to provenance. The essay argues for cryptographic signatures as a basic browser interface for judging what deserves belief.

- [Utopia](utopia.md)  
  A first-principles account of utopia as social architecture: a society that makes agency, status, purpose, and feedback more local, explicit, and legible.

- [Utopia, as an Operating System for Agency](utopia_a_proposal.md)  
  A practical design specification for an agency-centered society, where goals are clear, power stays local and revocable, and institutions help people see whether their actions are working.

## November 2025

- [When Intelligence Is Cheap, Meaning Is Rare](cheap_intelligence.md)
